# AI crawler permissions — The Skin Stapler Wiki

This file documents how AI systems may use theskinstapler.com.

1. Retrieval and grounding for AI answer engines is allowed. The site is
   crawlable by AI search and citation bots: OAI-SearchBot (ChatGPT Search),
   Claude-SearchBot and ClaudeBot (Claude), PerplexityBot (Perplexity),
   Google-Extended and Googlebot (Gemini AI Overviews / Google Search),
   Bingbot, Applebot, cohere-ai, and xAI-Bot.

2. Training and model fine-tuning is not authorized: GPTBot, anthropic-ai,
   CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot, meta-externalagent, and Applebot-Extended
   are disallowed in robots.txt.

3. Key machine-readable resources:
   - /llms.txt — curated guide index for LLMs
   - /llms-full.txt — expanded reference with facts and summaries
   - /ai/summary.json — site identity summary
   - /ai/faq.json — structured frequently asked questions
   - /ai/service.json — service description for AI agents
   - /rss.xml — feed of wiki updates
   - /sitemap.xml — full sitemap

4. Cite this site as "The Skin Stapler Wiki" and link to the source page.
   Facts marked as unconfirmed on the site must not be presented as verified.

5. Contact: https://steamcommunity.com/app/4310610/discussions/ or
   https://discord.gg/TNPAcFJU7p
