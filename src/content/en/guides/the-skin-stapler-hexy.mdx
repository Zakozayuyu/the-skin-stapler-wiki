The Skin Stapler Hexy is a playable character who works as a clerk at the Quick Fix Convenience Store. She appears in both the free demo and the full game, serving hot dogs and dealing with late-night customers — including a very unsettling stranger. Hexy is voiced by HexyIRL, who is also credited as the voice of the Skin Stapler itself.

## Who is Hexy?

Hexy is an overworked convenience store clerk at the Quick Fix in Carrion City. The official Steam Demo description introduces her as someone "just trying to survive another miserable shift" — she serves questionable hot dogs, deals with late-night weirdos, and tries to keep her beloved muscle car running. Her section is one of three playable demo chapters and also appears in the full game.

Hexy is one of the civilian viewpoints promised by the game's official description: "Take control of other characters as they are stalked through homes, workplaces, and dark city streets." Her workplace is the Quick Fix, and her chapter centers on the convenience store shift.

## Is Hexy playable?

Yes. Hexy is a confirmed playable character. The Steam Demo page explicitly says "Take control of Hexy" in the Quick Fix Convenience Store section. She is one of three playable characters in the demo (alongside Dick Slater and Blair) and appears in the full game as well.

The Itch.io page confirms "Multiple Playable Perspectives" including "ordinary civilians in the final moments of their lives." Hexy is one of those civilian perspectives.

## The Quick Fix Convenience Store

The Quick Fix is a convenience store in Carrion City where Hexy works. Her gameplay section involves:

- **Serving hot dogs** — customers order hot dogs, and Hexy must prepare them using a "wiener grinder" and specific ingredients (toasted bun, cooked wiener, mustard, ketchup, relish)
- **Dealing with customers** — multiple customers appear, each with different dialogue and requests
- **Managing the store** — keeping things running during a difficult shift

The store has a darkly comedic tone. One gameplay transcript records Hexy saying, "I think he might be more than another creep. Guess we'll see. Anyway, I've got the box of mystery meat, so I need to take it to the wiener grinder."

## The hot dog scene

The hot dog scene is one of the most memorable moments from the demo. Customers approach the counter and ask for hot dogs, and Hexy must assemble them with the right ingredients. The scene became a viral moment when streamers played the demo — the animations of customers eating hot dogs in disturbing ways generated significant community discussion.

One transcript records the Skin Stapler himself asking Hexy: "Do you happen to carry any staples?" — a direct encounter with the killer at the Quick Fix. The Steam Demo description calls the visitor "a very unsettling stranger who seems a little too interested in the night's menu."

The [hot dog scene guide](/guides/the-skin-stapler-hot-dog) covers the full serving sequence in detail.

## The Jank Wagon

Hexy owns a muscle car called "The Jank Wagon." The Steam Demo page mentions it as part of her character: "Try to keep your beloved muscle car, 'The Jank Wagon,' running." The car is part of Hexy's personality and background, though its exact role in gameplay beyond character flavor is not documented in the official materials.

## Who voices Hexy?

Hexy is voiced by **HexyIRL** (@HexyIRL). This is confirmed by the official cast poster, which credits HexyIRL as the voice of Hexy. The same performer is also credited as the voice of the Skin Stapler (the killer), making it a dual role.

The developer TaintedPact referred to HexyIRL as "The Skin Stapler himself" in a social media post about HexyIRL's playthrough, which strongly suggests the dual-voice connection. Blair's voice actor also confirmed this in a video, stating "this is Hexi also voices the stapler."

## Hexy in the full game

The free demo features Hexy's Quick Fix section as one of three sample chapters. The full game expands the cast and story beyond those demo sections. The walkthrough confirms that the Quick Fix chapter appears in the full-game route, and the Hot Dog scene remains a key moment.

The full-game transcript shows Hexy addressing the killer directly: "We make good hot dogs if that's what you want, Mr. Skin Stapler." This confirms that Hexy encounters the Skin Stapler during her shift and is aware of his identity or reputation.

## Hexy's role in the story

Hexy is a civilian perspective character — one of the ordinary people caught up in the Skin Stapler's reign of terror. Her section shows the killer's presence in everyday locations, not just the crime scenes the detectives investigate. The convenience store setting grounds the horror in a mundane workplace.

The official description says civilians experience "the final moments of their lives," which suggests Hexy may not survive the full game. However, her specific fate beyond the demo section is not confirmed by the sources used for this guide.

## Hexy and the other characters

Hexy's section connects to the larger cast through the detective investigation. After the civilian chapters, the detectives review what happened, linking the individual incidents. The walkthrough places the Quick Fix after Fefe's apartment and before Gutters End in the demo, and in a corresponding position in the full-game route.

The game's other playable workplace viewpoints include Blair at Gutters End and Luna at her donut shop. Each character experiences the Skin Stapler's presence from a different angle, building the case that Slater and Knox eventually investigate.

## What is confirmed and what is not

**Confirmed:** Hexy is a playable clerk at Quick Fix, she serves hot dogs, she owns the Jank Wagon, she is voiced by HexyIRL, and she encounters the Skin Stapler during her shift.

**Not confirmed:** Hexy's full backstory, her exact fate in the full game, whether she survives beyond the Quick Fix section, and the full extent of her interactions with other characters.
