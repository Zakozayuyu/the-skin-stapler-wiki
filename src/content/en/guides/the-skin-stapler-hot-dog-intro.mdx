- **What is the hot dog scene?** It's the Quick Fix shift in The Skin Stapler. You play Hexy, an overworked clerk serving questionable hot dogs to late-night customers. See the [Hexy character guide](/guides/the-skin-stapler-hexy).
- **Is Quick Fix in the demo and full game?** Yes. The [official Steam demo](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4343530/The_Skin_Stapler_Demo/) includes Quick Fix as one of three sections, and the released game also has a Quick Fix chapter. Steam's [Hexy - Wiener Whisperer](https://steamcommunity.com/achievements/4310610) achievement confirms the full-game chapter.
- **Is The Quick Fix a separate game?** No. It's a chapter inside The Skin Stapler, not a restaurant-management game or a standalone download.
