- **How many achievements are there?** The August 2026 release has 16 Steam achievements. Fifteen follow chapter progress, tape repair, or the final cutscene.
- **Which achievement is easiest to miss?** **Skin Successfully Stapled!** requires interacting with the “Thank You” note in Dick Slater's office. Steam currently shows a 29.0% global unlock rate.
- **Which achievement needs tape repair?** **Runebee - Jank Ya Later!** unlocks by fixing The Skin Stapler tapes. See the [tape guide](/guides/the-skin-stapler-all-tapes) for the recordings and repair context.
