- **Who is the killer?** Elias Crow, owner of the Flesh Pit, is the Skin Stapler. He targets Dick Slater because Slater killed his twin brother, Silus Kain. See the [full identity and motive guide](/guides/who-is-the-skin-stapler).
- **How is the story structured?** The game runs through 14 short chapters, switching between Slater and Robbie's investigation and the victims' viewpoints. Four Aftermath interludes and the Final Aftermath connect both sides. Follow the [chapter order](/walkthrough).
- **How does it end?** The main ending takes place at the Flesh Pit. Robbie is struck down, Slater shoots Elias, and Slater leaves Carrion City. The footage leaves Elias's fate open. Read the [ending explained](/ending).
