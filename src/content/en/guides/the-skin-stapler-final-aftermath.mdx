The Final Aftermath is the chapter of The Skin Stapler that closes out the victim stories — the fifth and last of the game's Aftermath interludes, placed after the last victim chapter in the route and before the two-chapter endgame. Completing it unlocks the Steam achievement "Nine, Ten, Never Jank Again.", and it completes the game's 14-chapter math: nine playable location chapters, four numbered Aftermath parts, and the Final Aftermath itself.

## The achievement behind the chapter

The Steam achievement reads "Nine, Ten, Never Jank Again." with the requirement "Complete The Final Aftermath Chapter." There is no hidden condition and no missable element to it: the achievement pops when the chapter is complete. Its name is the payoff of a counting rhyme the game runs across its interlude achievements — Aftermath Part 3 carries "Five, six, grab your crucifix...", and the sequence lands on "nine, ten" here, at the final interlude. If you are chasing a clean achievement list, this one is unmissable as long as you finish the route; the game's genuinely missable unlock is the office Thank You note, tracked separately in our [achievements guide](/guides/the-skin-stapler-achievements).

## What happens in the Final Aftermath

The Aftermath chapters exist to pull each victim's story back into Dick Slater's case file. The victim chapters are ordinary work shifts — serving hot dogs, filling donuts, rewinding tapes — that end in the killer's hands; the Aftermath parts then reframe those deaths as evidence, connections, and a count that keeps climbing. The Final Aftermath closes that thread: the last victim story folds into the investigation, and the case is complete enough for Slater to move on the killer.

What the chapter looks like moment to moment — a cutscene-style interlude or a section with its own interactions — is a detail to verify in-game. Official materials record the chapter's name, its position in the progression order, and its achievement, and nothing more granular than that. The [full walkthrough](/walkthrough) carries the chapter-by-chapter route with timestamps if you want the surrounding context.

## How it sets up the endgame

Two chapters remain after the Final Aftermath, and they are all investigation. Closing In, opening at 02:18:29 in the reference run, is where the case converges on a suspect. The Flesh Pit — Elias Crow's barbecue restaurant, and the answer to the game's title question — hosts the finale at 02:34:00. From the Final Aftermath onward there are no more victim chapters to play, no more shifts to work: the remaining twenty minutes are the story closing its jaws. A: The fifth and final Aftermath interlude — the chapter that closes the victim thread before the two-chapter endgame of Closing In and the Flesh Pit.

**Q: What achievement does it unlock?**

**Q: Is the Final Aftermath a playable chapter?**

**Q: How far into the game is it?**
A: Around the two-hour mark. Lazar's Blood Bank ends near 01:58:34 in the reference run, and Closing In opens at 02:18:29, with the interlude between them.

**Q: Does the game end after the Final Aftermath?**
