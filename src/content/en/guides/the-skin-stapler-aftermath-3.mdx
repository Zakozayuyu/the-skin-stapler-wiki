Aftermath 3 — the Steam achievement names it The Aftermath Part 3 — is the third of The Skin Stapler's four Aftermath interludes, and it sits in the middle of the victim-to-investigation handoff: after Luna's Cream Filled Donuts chapter and before the Underworld VHS chapter. Completing it unlocks the achievement "Five, six, grab your crucifix...", the third beat of the nursery-rhyme counting pattern the game runs across its interlude achievements.

## What the chapter does for the story

The Aftermath formula is the connective tissue of the game's structure. Each victim chapter ends the same way — an ordinary shift that turns into the killer's hands — and each Aftermath part then pulls that death back into Dick Slater's case file: the evidence boards grow, the connections multiply, the count climbs. Part 3 is where the back half of that rhythm locks in. It follows Luna's chapter in the progression order, and Luna's scene carries the VHS thread that the next location chapter runs with: Underworld VHS is Scaroline's store, and the connection between the donut shop and the video store is documented in our [who is The Skin Stapler](/guides/who-is-the-skin-stapler) guide.

The chapter's own scene-by-scene content — which victims it touches and how the case file frames them — is not separately documented in official materials. What the official record pins down is the chapter's name, its slot in the progression order, and its achievement, and those three facts are enough to place it exactly.

That placement also explains the achievement rhyme. The interludes carry the game's counting achievements in sequence — Part 3's "Five, six, grab your crucifix..." is the third line, and the set resolves with "Nine, Ten, Never Jank Again." on the Final Aftermath. Reading the achievement list top to bottom therefore reads the interlude order itself, which is how the route position above is pinned: the Steam achievement order places The Aftermath Part 3 directly between the Luna chapter's unlock and the Underworld VHS stretch that follows.

## How to reach Aftermath 3

1. **Complete Luna's Cream Filled Donuts.** Work the serving shift to its end — the chapter where Luna and Chief O'Brien meet their fate — and the story moves to the interlude automatically.
2. **Let the interlude play out.** The achievement "Five, six, grab your crucifix..." pops when the chapter completes; there is no choice, hidden condition, or collectible tied to the unlock.
3. **Continue into Underworld VHS.** The next location chapter opens at 01:34:10 in the reference run — Scaroline's video store, a roughly 24-minute playable chapter per the route timeline.

Knowing what surrounds the interlude helps you place it on the map. Before it, the route has played five location chapters, with Luna's Cream Filled Donuts as the fifth; after the video store, only Lazar's Blood Bank remains before the endgame chapters take over. Aftermath Part 3 is the hinge between the donut shop's closing beat and the video-store chapter that picks up the VHS thread Luna's scene plants — the moment the game stops introducing victims and starts closing the case.
