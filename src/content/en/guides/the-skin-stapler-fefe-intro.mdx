- **Who is Fefe?** She's the first playable character in the full game. Her opening takes place at the Burrows Apartments. See the [opening chapter in the walkthrough](/walkthrough#chapter-order) and the [chapter credit on Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/achievements/4310610).
- **Who voices Fefe?** FeFe, credited as @CovfefeChan, voices the character. See the [voice cast and its sources](/guides/the-skin-stapler-voice-actors).
- **Is Fefe in the demo?** Her apartment chapter is in the full game. The [free Steam demo](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4343530/The_Skin_Stapler_Demo/) lists three other playable sections, featuring Dick Slater, Hexy, and Blair.
