The Skin Stapler was made by Tainted Pact, a solo developer based in the United States, and published by Assemble Entertainment, the German publisher behind Sticky Business, Roadwarden, and the Endzone series. The game launched on August 6, 2026 for Windows PC at 14.99 euros on Steam, and it is the studio's fifth horror title.

## Who is Tainted Pact?

Tainted Pact is the solo studio behind five horror games, and the publisher's launch announcement describes the developer as a one-person operation based in California. The studio builds deliberately niche grindhouse horror: short runtimes, PSX-era low-poly visuals, fully voiced casts of content creators, and joke-forward slasher writing.

The studio keeps a small public footprint: an itch.io account under the name taintedpact88, and a YouTube channel at @TaintedPactGames that published Dev Log entries for The Skin Stapler in December 2025 — Dev Log #1 on December 1 and Dev Log #2 on December 2 — documenting the build in public before launch. The studio does not publish founder names or personal details; its public identity is the brand, the games, and the creator collaborations.

## The four games before The Skin Stapler

The back catalog is confirmed by both the publisher's announcement and the GOG catalog:

1. **Suffer the Night** — the studio's analog-horror debut built around a haunted retro computer.
2. **Terror at Oakheart** — a camp-set slasher with the same stylized violence.
3. **Massacre at the Mirage** — a hotel-bound slasher with scene-based pacing.
4. **Flesh Made Fear** — the direct predecessor: a serial-killer story with voiced scenes and a grindhouse edge.

Played in order, the catalog shows the format maturing: longer voice work, more polished FMV-style presentation, and increasingly elaborate structure, arriving at The Skin Stapler's 14-chapter, viewpoint-switching Carrion City story. The [gameplay guide](/guides/the-skin-stapler-gameplay) and the catalog order above are the fastest way to see that evolution if you want more from the same hand.

## The publisher: Assemble Entertainment

Assemble Entertainment is a German publisher founded in 2016, with more than 50 published games to its name. Its range is broader than horror: Sticky Business is a cozy smash hit, Roadwarden is a critically praised narrative RPG, and the Endzone series anchors its survival-strategy side. The Skin Stapler sits in its grindhouse lane, alongside the publisher's work with short-form horror projects.

For players, the publisher matters in practical ways: the launch press push, the Tiny Teams Steam event placement during the demo window, and the storefront presence on Steam and GOG all run through Assemble. The store listing carries the Assemble Entertainment GmbH copyright line, and the publisher's announcement is the source for the launch facts above.

## The creator-cast production model

The publisher describes the game as built with support from some of your favorite content creators, and the cast poster backs it with ten named performers: RJ Green, Daniel Suraci, HexyIRL, Layna Lazar, Runebee, Luna Arcana, Blairvira, FeFe, Scaroline, and KingWoolz. Several characters are modeled on and voiced by the creators themselves — Scaroline and Underworld VHS are the clearest example.

That model is the studio's signature. It turns a solo project into a collaborative launch, gives the game a built-in audience through the creators' channels, and keeps the fully voiced presentation affordable for a 2.5-hour game.
