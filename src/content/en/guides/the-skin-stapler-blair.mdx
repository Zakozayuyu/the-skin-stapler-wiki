The Skin Stapler Blair is a playable character who works the graveyard shift at Gutters End Bowling Alley. She appears in the free demo and the full game, completing small tasks before a threatening figure walks through the door. Blair is voiced by BlairVira (@Blairvira), who also voices Mr. Shrimp in a dual role.

## Who is Blair?

Blair is a tired employee working the graveyard shift at Gutters End Bowling Alley in Carrion City. The official Steam Demo description introduces her section: "Step into the shoes of Blair, a tired employee working the graveyard shift at a run-down bowling alley. Complete small tasks, keep the place running, and try to survive just another ordinary night… Until a familiar face walks through the door."

Blair is one of three playable characters in the free demo (alongside Dick Slater and Hexy) and also appears in the full game. Her section is part of the game's multiple-perspective structure, showing another civilian caught in the Skin Stapler's path.

## Is Blair playable?

Yes. Blair is a confirmed playable character. The Steam Demo explicitly says "Step into the shoes of Blair," confirming player control. She is one of the three demo sections and appears in the full-game route as well.

The Itch.io page describes "Multiple Playable Perspectives" including "ordinary civilians in the final moments of their lives." Blair is one of those perspectives.

## Gutters End Bowling Alley

Gutters End Bowling Alley is Blair's workplace in Carrion City. Her section involves:

- **Completing small tasks** — keeping the bowling alley running during the graveyard shift
- **Cleaning bowling balls** — maintaining the equipment
- **Spraying deodorant in shoes** — a specific gameplay task that community members noted as memorable
- **Dealing with coworkers** — including Lucas, a fellow employee

The bowling alley has signage with rules: "No running in the bowling alley… no food or drink in the bowling area." These details come from community-shared gameplay clips and confirm the location's atmosphere.

## What happens to Blair?

The demo ends with Blair's death. The Steam Demo description foreshadows this with "Until a familiar face walks through the door" — the familiar face being the Skin Stapler. Full-game transcripts confirm that Blair is killed and her face is stapled, following the killer's signature method.

One transcript records the player reacting: "That better not be her face. I swear to God. Oh my god. That is Blair's face." Another transcript has the player saying, "Now I know why he's called the skin stapler" — connecting the stapling method to Blair's fate.

The full-game walkthrough confirms that after Blair's section, the detectives review what happened, placing her events within the larger investigation.

## Who voices Blair?

Blair is voiced by **BlairVira** (@Blairvira). This is confirmed by:

- The official cast poster, which credits "Blair - @Blairvira"
- BlairVira's own Instagram bio: "twitch partner | youtube partner aspiring voice actor. Caseoh play as my character in The Skin Stapler"
- BlairVira's Twitter post: "My first playable character & first time voice acting"
- A video titled "I'M IN THIS GAME (as myself & a shrimp)"

BlairVira describes the role as her first playable character and first voice acting work. She also expressed that CaseOh playing as her character was a career highlight.

## Mr. Shrimp: the dual role

Blair's voice actor also voices **Mr. Shrimp**, a character at Gutters End Bowling Alley. BlairVira confirmed this in multiple posts:

- YouTube video title: "I'M IN THIS GAME (as myself & a shrimp)"
- Instagram reel: "Mr. Shrimp & I (both voiced by me)"
- Gameplay transcript: "this is Hexi also voices the stapler" — same video also discusses the shrimp role

Mr. Shrimp appears to be a companion or side character in the Gutters End section. One transcript records dialogue: "Mr. Shrimp, you're supposed to be in my belly. We don't have time for this, Blair." The voice actor also mentioned refining the shrimp voice for the full game release.

## Blair's connection to other characters

Blair's section connects to the larger story through the detective investigation. The walkthrough places Gutters End after Quick Fix in the demo route, and the full-game transcript shows the detectives reviewing what happened at each location.

One transcript records a player asking: "What I want to know is how did Blair and the hot dog guy…" — suggesting that Blair and Hexy (the hot dog clerk) are connected or referenced across sections. The characters exist in the same city and their incidents are investigated together.

The walkthrough confirms that after Blair's section, "the detectives review what happened," placing her events within Slater's investigation.

## Blair in the demo vs full game

The free demo includes Blair's Gutters End section as one of three sample chapters. The full game expands the story beyond the demo. Blair's death in the demo appears to be part of the full-game narrative as well, based on the walkthrough chapter order.

The demo launched in January 2026, months before the full game arrived in August 2026. The voice actor's video about refining the shrimp voice suggests that the full game may have expanded or polished the Gutters End section beyond the demo version.

## What is confirmed and what is not

**Confirmed:** Blair is a playable graveyard-shift employee at Gutters End Bowling Alley, voiced by BlairVira, who also voices Mr. Shrimp. She is killed by the Skin Stapler in both the demo and full game.

**Not confirmed:** Blair's full backstory beyond her workplace role, whether she appears elsewhere in the game before or after her death section, and the exact meaning of the "Blair mask" reference found in one transcript.
