## Who is Fefe?

Fefe is a named character in the full cast, not an alternate spelling of another protagonist. The developer’s official cast poster maps “Fefe” to @CovfefeChan, and Tainted Pact’s announcement thanks the performer as Fefe. The Steam page does not list the character by name because it only identifies Dick Slater, Robbie Knox, and unnamed other viewpoints.

Official store copy still supports Fefe’s general role by saying players control people stalked through homes, workplaces, and dark city streets. A media writer who played the release version specifically describes Fefe alone in her apartment. The combination supports a home-based playable opening without requiring an invented biography.

## Does Fefe open the game?

Kyusai Media’s August 6 first impression says Fefe is the girl the player starts as. The scene places her in an apartment during a thunderstorm, waking for “midnight matcha” before things go wrong. That is a first-hand media observation rather than a line from the Steam description.

The claim is compatible with the official statement that Dick Slater is the primary protagonist. The [full walkthrough](/walkthrough) places the Carrion City Police Department after the apartment opening, while the free demo begins with its own three-section sample. Fefe can open the released game without replacing Slater as the main detective.

## Who voices Fefe?

FeFe, publicly credited as @CovfefeChan, voices the role. Tainted Pact announced on June 3 that she had joined the cast, and FeFe posted on the same day that players would hear her voice a character in The Skin Stapler. The official cast poster later supplied the direct Fefe mapping.

Her public biography describes her as a punk-rock VTuber and voice actress. No public source used for this profile provides a legal name, age, location, or other personal data. This article retains the credited stage name and handle instead of speculating about identity.

## Why the cast announcement came late

The June announcement arrived roughly two months before the August 6 release. Tainted Pact thanked FeFe for helping “out of a bind,” but did not explain the production issue. The wording may indicate a late casting change, but the reason has not been confirmed.

An older indexed cast-model list reportedly included @wolfatyourgate and omitted @CovfefeChan. The page itself was inaccessible, and its date could not be verified. The final official cast poster is therefore the stronger source for Fefe’s released credit.

## Fefe’s place in the larger story

The central investigation follows Dick Slater and Robbie Knox as they hunt the serial killer called The Skin Stapler. Other playable viewpoints show people being stalked before or around the detectives’ work. Fefe’s apartment scene fits the official promise of a home-based victim perspective.

The developer announcement says the Stapler would be hunting her that summer. It does not state whether Fefe dies, escapes, meets the detectives, or returns later. None of those outcomes is stated in the character sources used for this profile.

## Fefe and the free demo

The official free demo lists only three sections: CCPD with Dick Slater, Quick Fix with Hexy, and Gutters End with Blair. Fefe is not named in that demo description. The media report about her opening role refers to the full game released in August.

That difference shows that the full release begins outside the three sections advertised for the January demo. No official comparison explains whether Fefe’s scene was added later or simply omitted from the sample description, so the two openings should not be treated as a contradiction.

## What is Fefe’s Steam achievement?

The [official Steam achievement list](https://steamcommunity.com/achievements/4310610) calls it **FeFe - Master Baiter**. It unlocks when you complete the Burrows Apartment chapter. The “Fifi” spelling in an automated transcript differs from Steam's published credit.

## Voice language and presentation

Steam lists English interface, full audio, and subtitles, and says every character is fully voiced. Fefe’s confirmed performance is therefore part of the English voice track. No official performer for another-language dub appears on the pages checked here.

The character is presented within the game’s low-poly PSX grindhouse style. Official copy combines serious gore with dark B-movie comedy, and the media impression describes Fefe’s scene in that same tonal context. Her exact model details, animations, and dialogue count are not documented publicly.

## What is verified and what is not

Verified points are limited but clear: Fefe is a cast-poster character, @CovfefeChan voices her, Tainted Pact announced the performer on June 3, and a release-day media impression identifies Fefe as the opening playable viewpoint. Steam independently confirms a fully voiced, multi-perspective story. Those sources reinforce one another without supplying a complete character arc.

The character-specific sources cover the opening and casting. Details about her background and later appearances remain unconfirmed in those sources.
