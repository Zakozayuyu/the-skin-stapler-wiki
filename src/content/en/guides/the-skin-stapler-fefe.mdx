## Who is Fefe?

Fefe is a named character in the full cast, not an alternate spelling of another protagonist. The developer’s official cast poster maps “Fefe” to @CovfefeChan, and Tainted Pact’s announcement thanks the performer as Fefe. The Steam page does not list the character by name because it only identifies Dick Slater, Robbie Knox, and unnamed other viewpoints.

Official store copy still supports Fefe’s general role by saying players control people stalked through homes, workplaces, and dark city streets. A media writer who played the release version specifically describes Fefe alone in her apartment. The combination supports a home-based playable opening without requiring an invented biography.

## Does Fefe open the game?

Kyusai Media’s August 6 first impression says Fefe is the girl the player starts as. The scene places her in an apartment during a thunderstorm, waking for “midnight matcha” before things go wrong. That is a first-hand media observation rather than a line from the Steam description.

The claim is compatible with the official statement that Dick Slater is the primary protagonist. The [full walkthrough](/walkthrough) places the Carrion City Police Department after the apartment opening, while the free demo begins with its own three-section sample. Fefe can open the released game without replacing Slater as the main detective.

## Who voices Fefe?

FeFe, publicly credited as @CovfefeChan, voices the role. Tainted Pact announced on June 3 that she had joined the cast, and FeFe posted on the same day that players would hear her voice a character in The Skin Stapler. The official cast poster later supplied the direct Fefe mapping.

Her public biography describes her as a punk-rock VTuber and voice actress. No public source used for this profile provides a legal name, age, location, or other personal data. This article retains the credited stage name and handle instead of speculating about identity.

## Why the cast announcement came late

The June announcement arrived roughly two months before the August 6 release. Tainted Pact thanked FeFe for helping “out of a bind,” but did not explain the production issue. The wording may indicate a late casting change, but the reason has not been confirmed.

An older indexed cast-model list reportedly included @wolfatyourgate and omitted @CovfefeChan. The page itself was inaccessible, and its date could not be verified. The final official cast poster is therefore the stronger source for Fefe’s released credit.
