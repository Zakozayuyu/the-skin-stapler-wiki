## Who is Scaroline?

Scaroline is a named Carrion City character associated with Underworld VHS. Tainted Pact describes the place as a grimy, sleazy, old-school VHS rental store and identifies Scaroline as its owner. That direct developer statement is the strongest source for her in-game occupation.

The Steam storefront does not name Scaroline or Underworld VHS in its short cast description. It does, however, promise multiple characters, workplaces, and a story built with support from content creators. The separate official reveal supplies the specific character detail missing from the general store copy.

## Who voices and inspired Scaroline?

The character and model are based on @Scarolinetv_, according to Tainted Pact. The creator’s public profile describes a horror-focused live show involving scary games, movies, collecting unusual items, and voice acting. The official cast poster confirms that the same creator voices Scaroline.

This is more than a same-name fan assumption because both the developer announcement and official poster make the connection. Scaroline also reposted the reveal with an enthusiastic response. This guide does not infer private identity details that are not part of the public credit.

## What is Underworld VHS?

Underworld VHS is an old-school video-rental store in Carrion City. Its name and presentation fit the game’s 1980s VHS, grindhouse, and noir influences. The supplied official post confirms the location’s existence but does not publish a full objective list.

Underworld VHS is full-game content and is not one of the three sections listed for the free demo. The released chapter route places it after Luna’s shop and before Lazar’s Blood Bank, while its exact length and task list are not published by the storefront.

Tainted Pact introduced Scaroline in a post calling it another “playable character reveal.” Taken literally, that wording confirms some playable status. A later fan wiki, however, says Scaroline is not marketed as another civilian shift in the same mold as Hexy or Blair and treats her more as the face of the store.

Those statements may describe different scales of control rather than a direct contradiction. Scaroline can be associated with a viewpoint chapter without the character announcement defining how long the player controls her. This guide therefore avoids claiming a specific duration or task structure.

## Is Scaroline in the demo?

The free Steam demo names three playable sections and three leads: Dick Slater at CCPD, Hexy at Quick Fix, and Blair at Gutters End. It does not list Underworld VHS or Scaroline. The demo launched in January, months before the full game arrived in August.

Scaroline is not advertised as demo content. The demo page lists only CCPD, Quick Fix, and Gutters End, while the released route places Underworld VHS later in the full game. There is no evidence of a separate secret demo cameo.
