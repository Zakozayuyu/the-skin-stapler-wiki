The Skin Stapler Scaroline is the owner of Underworld VHS and is based on and voiced by creator @Scarolinetv_. Tainted Pact revealed the character and model connection, while the developer’s cast poster credits “Scaroline - @Scarolinetv_.” The released game also gives her a playable viewpoint chapter: the [full walkthrough](/walkthrough) places Underworld VHS at 01:34:10, between Luna’s donut shop and Lazar’s Blood Bank.

## Who is Scaroline?

Scaroline is a named Carrion City character associated with Underworld VHS. Tainted Pact describes the place as a grimy, sleazy, old-school VHS rental store and identifies Scaroline as its owner. That direct developer statement is the strongest source for her in-game occupation.

The Steam storefront does not name Scaroline or Underworld VHS in its short cast description. It does, however, promise multiple characters, workplaces, and a story built with support from content creators. The separate official reveal supplies the specific character detail missing from the general store copy.

## Who voices and inspired Scaroline?

The character and model are based on @Scarolinetv_, according to Tainted Pact. The creator’s public profile describes a horror-focused live show involving scary games, movies, collecting unusual items, and voice acting. The official cast poster confirms that the same creator voices Scaroline.

This is more than a same-name fan assumption because both the developer announcement and official poster make the connection. Scaroline also reposted the reveal with an enthusiastic response. This guide does not infer private identity details that are not part of the public credit.

## What is Underworld VHS?

Underworld VHS is an old-school video-rental store in Carrion City. Its name and presentation fit the game’s 1980s VHS, grindhouse, and noir influences. Tainted Pact’s reveal confirms the location, and full-game footage adds the chapter work: rewinding tapes at the counter is part of Scaroline’s section before its story beat lands.

Underworld VHS is full-game content and is not one of the three sections listed for the free demo. The released chapter route places it after Luna’s shop and before Lazar’s Blood Bank, and the reference walkthrough spans roughly 24 minutes between the 01:34:10 and 01:58:34 chapter marks.

## Is Scaroline playable?

Yes. The released game gives Scaroline a playable viewpoint chapter at Underworld VHS. Three sources line up: a chaptered full-game walkthrough labels 01:34:10 as the Underworld VHS chapter with Scaroline as the viewpoint, the site’s [characters database](/characters) lists her as that chapter’s perspective, and subtitles on Scaroline’s own video refer to playing her section in the store.

The chapter plays as a workplace shift in the same mold as Hexy’s Quick Fix or Luna’s donut shop: Scaroline handles tape-rewinding work at the counter before the segment’s story beat lands. Her sequence is not one of the three demo sections, and the developer’s original “playable character reveal” wording describes this full-game chapter rather than a cameo.

## Is Scaroline in the demo?

The free Steam demo names three playable sections and three leads: Dick Slater at CCPD, Hexy at Quick Fix, and Blair at Gutters End. It does not list Underworld VHS or Scaroline. The demo launched in January, months before the full game arrived in August.

Scaroline is not advertised as demo content. The demo page lists only CCPD, Quick Fix, and Gutters End, while the released route places Underworld VHS later in the full game. There is no evidence of a separate secret demo cameo.

## Scaroline and the cast

The official poster places Scaroline among eleven named role credits. Other mappings include RJ Green as Dick Slater, Daniel Suraci as Robbie Knox, Layna Lazar as Nurse Layna, and @HexyIRL as both Hexy and the Skin Stapler. This establishes Scaroline as part of the fully voiced cast rather than only a visual likeness.

Steam lists English for interface, full audio, and subtitles. No additional dub performer for Scaroline appears on the official pages checked here.

## The Tape Collector achievement

A fan wiki first reported a Steam achievement framed around “Scaroline - The Tape Collector,” and the official Steam achievement list now confirms it. The achievement unlocks for completing The Underworld VHS Chapter, which independently confirms that Scaroline’s store is a named full-game chapter.

That makes the achievement a progress marker rather than DLC or a separate Scaroline mode. The site’s [achievements guide](/guides/the-skin-stapler-achievements) lists all 16 unlocks with their Steam requirements, including this one.

## How Scaroline fits the tone

The character’s VHS-store setting directly echoes the game’s retro presentation. Official descriptions draw on VHS slashers, exploitation cinema, noir crime drama, low-poly graphics, and B-movie humor. A creator known for horror and movie discussion is a natural fit for that setting, although this is interpretation rather than a plot fact.

The larger story still centers on Slater’s hunt for the serial killer. Scaroline’s relationship to that investigation, any encounter with the killer, and her final outcome are not stated in the official character material, so this profile does not guess them.

## The confirmed profile

Scaroline owns Underworld VHS, and the character is based on and voiced by @Scarolinetv_. Tainted Pact made that connection publicly, and the developer’s official cast poster repeated the voice credit. The location is associated with the full game rather than the three advertised demo sections.

The resolved point is that Scaroline is both the store’s owner and the playable viewpoint of its chapter. The remaining unknowns are smaller: the task list beyond the tape work and the precise ties between her segment and the wider investigation are not detailed in official material.

The official reveal is authoritative for her ownership of Underworld VHS and her connection to @Scarolinetv_.
