The Skin Stapler voice actors provide English performances for every character. A developer cast poster, reproduced consistently by two independent fan references, maps the principal voice cast roles to RJ Green, Daniel Suraci, HexyIRL, Layna Lazar, Runebee, Luna Arcana, Blairvira, FeFe, Scaroline, and KingWoolz. Steam separately confirms full English voice audio. For character profiles, locations, and playable status, see the [all characters hub](/characters).

## Dick Slater and Robbie Knox

RJ Green is credited as Detective Dick Slater, the burned-out investigator at the center of the story. Green’s professional website also lists The Skin Stapler among recent projects, providing a second source for his involvement. Steam names Slater as the lead but does not show cast credits on the store page.

Daniel Suraci is credited as rookie partner Robbie Knox. Suraci also has earlier credits with Tainted Pact, but that previous collaboration is background rather than proof of his current role. The official cast poster is the direct evidence for Robbie Knox.

## Hexy and The Skin Stapler

The poster assigns both Hexy and the title killer to @HexyIRL. Hexy is the overworked clerk controlled in the Quick Fix demo section, where the official description says she serves questionable hot dogs. The killer is the figure pursued by Slater and Knox across Carrion City.

The cast poster confirms the Hexy connection and also adds the killer credit. It does not give a separate biography or real name for @HexyIRL, so this guide does not speculate about either detail.

## Nurse Layna, Bee, and Luna

Layna Lazar is credited as Nurse Layna, a playable character announced by Tainted Pact. The announcement says Nurse Layna runs Lazar’s Blood Bank, and Layna Lazar confirmed the role on her own account. Her public biography describes her as a New Zealand-based cozy-horror VTuber and voice actor.

The cast poster credits Bee to @Runebee and Luna to @_LunaArcana_. Those role mappings are confirmed, but the official store description does not explain Bee’s plot function or Luna’s full story. Their relationships and outcomes have not been confirmed.

## Blair and Fefe

Blair is credited to @Blairvira. Blair appears in the free demo as the playable employee at Gutters End Bowling Alley, and Blairvira published a video whose title says she is in the game as herself and a shrimp. The poster supplies the direct role mapping that earlier community research lacked.

Fefe is credited to @CovfefeChan, also known publicly as FeFe. Tainted Pact announced FeFe’s addition to the cast on June 3, 2026, and the performer posted a role announcement on the same day. A release-day media impression identifies Fefe as the playable opening character in an apartment during a thunderstorm.

## Scaroline and DJ Woolz

Scaroline is credited to @Scarolinetv_. Tainted Pact separately announced that the character and model were based on the creator and described Scaroline as the owner of Underworld VHS, and the released game makes her the playable viewpoint of the Underworld VHS chapter, placed at 01:34:10 in the reference walkthrough.

DJ Woolz is credited to @KingWoolz. Full-game footage places him at his radio station during the late-game Closing In stretch, where Slater brings the damaged evidence tapes to be repaired, and Steam ties his name to “The Last Nut Standing” achievement for completing The Closing In chapter. He appears as a station character rather than a separately playable viewpoint.

## Why older cast lists differ

One community completion post listed ten performers, while an indexed Threads summary of an earlier official model post included @wolfatyourgate and omitted @CovfefeChan. FeFe’s cast announcement came on June 3 and thanked her for helping “out of a bind,” which fits the timing of the addition. The date and final status of @wolfatyourgate in that older post remain unconfirmed because the Threads page is inaccessible.

The final cast poster provides the clearest role mapping and is used as the primary list. Two independent reproductions plus developer and performer posts support several individual credits. No accessible final credit currently connects @wolfatyourgate to a released role.

## Language and full voice support

Steam says every character is fully voiced. Its language table lists English for interface, full audio, and subtitles, with no other official audio language listed. That means translated site interfaces do not imply translated game voice tracks.

No official dubbing cast for German, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, or another language appears on the official pages. Check the Steam language table for later additions rather than assuming a translated site interface means the game has another dub.

## What is still missing

The game’s end-credit sequence was not directly captured, and the IMDb and MobyGames pages for the game are inaccessible. The cast poster fills most role mappings, but it does not claim to list incidental voices, replacement credits, or people thanked outside the principal cast. A claim that the list contains every spoken extra would therefore go beyond what the poster shows.

The confirmed source remains the developer’s official cast-poster page, supported by Tainted Pact announcements, performer posts, and Steam’s full-voice statement. Character biographies not published in those sources are intentionally omitted. This keeps the cast guide useful without turning handles, likenesses, or community assumptions into personal facts.

Searches for “who voices Roger in The Skin Stapler” do not match a confirmed character on the official cast list. Results for unrelated performers named Roger belong to other games or films and should not be folded into this cast. If a later credit introduces a Roger, that would require a new official or in-game source.

## Is this the complete end-credit cast?

The table contains eleven named character credits because HexyIRL performs two roles. It should not be read as proof that the game has no incidental voices, cameos, or people thanked outside the principal poster. A complete official transcription of the scrolling end credits was not available.

Any later patch could also add or recut dialogue. Until a full official credit list is available, “every confirmed role” means every mapping supported by the cast poster, not every voice sound heard in the game.

## How cast evidence was prioritized

The official cast poster is used for role-to-performer mapping because it names both sides of each credit. Developer announcements and performer posts are used to confirm individual participation, while Steam establishes the fully voiced English presentation. Community completion posts are helpful cross-checks but are not allowed to replace a direct mapping.

This source hierarchy resolves several earlier ambiguities. Blairvira and HexyIRL were once linked to same-named characters mainly through handles and community comments, but the poster now gives explicit credits. The @wolfatyourgate discrepancy remains unresolved because no accessible final credit connects that handle to a released role.
