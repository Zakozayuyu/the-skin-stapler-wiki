The Skin Stapler voice actors are listed most clearly on the developer’s official cast poster. The poster credits RJ Green, Daniel Suraci, Layna Lazar, FeFe, Blairvira, Scaroline, HexyIRL, Runebee, Luna Arcana, and KingWoolz across eleven named roles. Steam confirms that every character is fully voiced and that the available full audio is English.

## Confirmed role and performer list

The official poster credits Dick Slater to @RJGreenVA and Robbie Knox to @Daniel_Suraci. It credits The Skin Stapler and Hexy to @HexyIRL, giving that performer two named roles. These mappings are stronger than earlier fan guesses because they come from the developer’s merchandise page.

The same poster lists Nurse Layna as @LaynaLazar, Bee as @Runebee, Luna as @_LunaArcana_, Blair as @Blairvira, Fefe as @CovfefeChan, Scaroline as @Scarolinetv_, and DJ Woolz as @KingWoolz. The supplied research does not provide legal names for creators who are credited by handles. This article keeps the public credit format rather than inventing personal details.

## Dick Slater and Robbie Knox

RJ Green is credited as Detective Dick Slater, the burned-out investigator at the center of the story. Green’s professional website also lists The Skin Stapler among recent projects, providing a second source for his involvement. Steam names Slater as the lead but does not show cast credits on the store page.

Daniel Suraci is credited as rookie partner Robbie Knox. The research also notes Suraci’s earlier work with Tainted Pact, but that previous collaboration is background rather than proof of his current role. The official cast poster is the direct evidence for Robbie Knox.

## Hexy and The Skin Stapler

The poster assigns both Hexy and the title killer to @HexyIRL. Hexy is the overworked clerk controlled in the Quick Fix demo section, where the official description says she serves questionable hot dogs. The killer is the figure pursued by Slater and Knox across Carrion City.

Earlier research could only infer the Hexy connection from a matching handle. The poster resolves that uncertainty and also adds the killer credit. The supplied material does not give a separate biography or real name for @HexyIRL, so those details remain **Pending confirmation**.

## Nurse Layna, Bee, and Luna

Layna Lazar is credited as Nurse Layna, a playable character announced by Tainted Pact. The announcement says Nurse Layna runs Lazar’s Blood Bank, and Layna Lazar confirmed the role on her own account. Her public biography describes her as a New Zealand-based cozy-horror VTuber and voice actor.

The cast poster credits Bee to @Runebee and Luna to @_LunaArcana_. Those role mappings are confirmed at poster level, but the supplied official store description does not explain either character’s plot function. Their chapters, relationships, and outcomes remain **Pending confirmation**.

## Blair and Fefe

Blair is credited to @Blairvira. Blair appears in the free demo as the playable employee at Gutters End Bowling Alley, and Blairvira published a video whose title says she is in the game as herself and a shrimp. The poster supplies the direct role mapping that earlier community research lacked.

Fefe is credited to @CovfefeChan, also known publicly as FeFe. Tainted Pact announced FeFe’s addition to the cast on June 3, 2026, and the performer posted a role announcement on the same day. A release-day media impression identifies Fefe as the playable opening character in an apartment during a thunderstorm.

## Scaroline and DJ Woolz

Scaroline is credited to @Scarolinetv_. Tainted Pact separately announced that the character and model were based on the creator and described Scaroline as the owner of Underworld VHS. That direct developer statement supports both the character credit and the creator connection.

DJ Woolz is credited to @KingWoolz. The supplied research confirms the name through the poster and collected gameplay material, but it does not provide a complete official biography for the character. The amount of dialogue and the exact chapter placement remain **Pending confirmation**.

## Why older cast lists differ

One community completion post listed ten performers, while an indexed Threads summary of an earlier official model post included @wolfatyourgate and omitted @CovfefeChan. FeFe’s cast announcement came on June 3 and thanked her for helping “out of a bind,” which may explain the change. The date and final status of @wolfatyourgate in that older post could not be verified because the Threads page was inaccessible.

The official cast poster provides the cleanest final role mapping in the supplied data and is therefore used as the primary list. Whether @wolfatyourgate has an additional unlisted final-game credit remains **Pending confirmation**. The article does not merge the two lists into an invented twelve-person cast.

## Language and full voice support

Steam says every character is fully voiced. Its language table lists English for interface, full audio, and subtitles, with no other official audio language in the collected snapshot. That means translated site interfaces do not imply translated game voice tracks.

No official dubbing cast for German, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, or another language appears in the supplied research. Fan dubs and subtitle patches remain **Pending confirmation**. Check the Steam language table for any later additions.

## What is still missing

The game’s end-credit sequence was not directly captured, and IMDb and MobyGames were inaccessible during research. The cast poster fills most role mappings, but it may not list incidental voices, replacement credits, or people thanked outside the principal cast. A claim that the list contains every spoken extra would therefore go beyond the evidence.

The confirmed source remains the developer’s official cast-poster page, supported by Tainted Pact announcements, performer posts, and Steam’s full-voice statement. Character biographies not published in those sources are intentionally omitted. This keeps the cast guide useful without turning handles, likenesses, or community assumptions into personal facts.

## How cast evidence was prioritized

The official cast poster is used for role-to-performer mapping because it names both sides of each credit. Developer announcements and performer posts are used to confirm individual participation, while Steam establishes the fully voiced English presentation. Community completion posts are helpful cross-checks but are not allowed to replace a direct mapping.

This hierarchy resolves several earlier ambiguities. Blairvira and HexyIRL were once linked to same-named characters mainly through handles and community comments, but the poster now supplies explicit credits. The remaining @wolfatyourgate discrepancy stays **Pending confirmation** because no accessible final credit connects that handle to a released role.
