## Why there is no official Korean patch

The Skin Stapler is a fully voice-acted FMV horror game with ten named performers on its cast poster. A Korean version would require two things at once: translated subtitles for every dialogue line and a full Korean dub recorded over the game's VHS-style video sequences. That is a heavy localization load for a solo developer — Tainted Pact built the game in about a year, and publisher Assemble Entertainment launched it at 14.99 euros on August 6, 2026 with an English-only store presentation.

Short, voice-heavy horror games from small teams routinely launch English-only, and Korean support, when it comes, typically shows up as a post-launch announcement in the store news feed. No such announcement exists for this game as of September 2026.
