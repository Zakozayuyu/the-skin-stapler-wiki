- **Is the demo free?** Yes. It is a separate free app on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4343530/The_Skin_Stapler_Demo/), with its own app ID from the full game.
- **How long is the demo?** Steam gives an official playtime of 30–45 minutes. Recorded runs often take 40–55 minutes, depending on exploration and commentary.
- **What does it include?** Three playable sections: the Carrion City Police Department with Dick Slater, Quick Fix with Hexy, and Gutters End Bowling Alley with Blair.
