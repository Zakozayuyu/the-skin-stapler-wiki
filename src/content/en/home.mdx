**The Skin Stapler** is a single-player retro horror adventure developed by **Tainted Pact** and published by **Assemble Entertainment**. Set in a decaying 1980s Carrion City, it follows veteran detective **Dick Slater** and rookie **Robbie Knox** as they investigate a serial killer whose murders are accompanied by disturbing displays and distorted audio tapes.

The game emphasizes atmosphere, story and tension over traditional survival mechanics. Players investigate crime scenes, solve puzzles and switch into other playable perspectives across homes, workplaces and city streets, all presented with PSX-style visuals, full English voice acting, grindhouse gore and intentionally dark B-movie humor.
