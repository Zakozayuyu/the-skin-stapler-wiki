**The Skin Stapler** is a single-player PSX-styled grindhouse horror adventure developed by **Tainted Pact** and published by **Assemble Entertainment**. Set in a decaying 1980s city, it combines first-person investigation, puzzle solving, dark comedy, and violent slasher horror.

Players primarily follow detective **Dick Slater** while also controlling other characters hunted across homes, workplaces, and city streets. The handcrafted story is designed as a focused horror feature with full English voice acting, multiple perspectives, **16 Steam achievements**, and a separate free demo.
