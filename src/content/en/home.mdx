**The Skin Stapler** is a single-player, first-person horror adventure developed by **Tainted Pact** and published by **Assemble Entertainment**. Its PSX-style visuals and 1980s VHS slasher influences frame a serial-killer investigation in **Carrion City**. You play burned-out detective **Dick Slater** and other residents whose everyday routines become part of the case. The full game released for Windows PC on **August 6, 2026**, with English dialogue, interface and subtitles. These release details come from the [official Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4310610/The_Skin_Stapler/).

### Carrion City's story and setting

Slater and rookie partner **Robbie Knox** follow a murderer who leaves mutilated victims and distorted audio recordings behind. The story moves between the police investigation and people living or working in the city, letting you experience a location before the detectives examine what happened there. The tapes connect individual scenes to the larger mystery.

Neon-lit streets, worn-out workplaces and grotesque humor give the setting its grindhouse tone. Follow the investigation's full plot in the [story guide](/guides/the-skin-stapler-story) and the final confrontation in [ending explained](/ending). Both pages mark major spoilers.

### How the game plays

The investigation mixes exploring small locations, examining crime scenes, conversations and environmental puzzles. Civilian chapters change the immediate objective: a counter needs service, a shift needs finishing, or a character needs to get through an increasingly threatening encounter. The changing viewpoints connect the residents' encounters to Slater's investigation.

The [developer's description](https://taintedpact88.itch.io/the-skin-stapler) presents the game as a short horror experience designed for one sitting, emphasizing atmosphere, story and tension over combat or traditional survival mechanics. Our [gameplay guide](/guides/the-skin-stapler-gameplay) explains that structure, while the [full walkthrough](/walkthrough) puts objectives in chapter order.

### Playable characters and the voice cast

The police-station scenes establish Slater's investigation. **Hexy** brings you into the Quick Fix convenience store, where serving hot dogs is part of an uncomfortable late shift. **Blair** works at Gutters End Bowling Alley, another ordinary workplace caught in the city's nightmare. These three perspectives are also the focus of the official demo.

The full-game route adds **Fefe**, **Luna**, **Scaroline** and **Nurse Layna**, with apartment, donut-shop, video-store and blood-bank scenes. Browse the [character profiles](/characters) for their roles and locations, or the [voice actors guide](/guides/the-skin-stapler-voice-actors) for performer credits. The cast is fully voiced in English.

### The full game and free demo

The [free Steam demo](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4343530/The_Skin_Stapler_Demo/) launched on **January 30, 2026** and offers an official estimate of **30–45 minutes**. It contains three playable sections: Carrion City Police Department, Quick Fix and Gutters End. That sample introduces detective work and the civilian shift sequences before you commit to the full story.

The paid release continues beyond those sections into later locations and the finale. Use the [demo guide](/guides/the-skin-stapler-demo) to check the sample's contents and the [chapter walkthrough](/walkthrough) when playing the complete game.
